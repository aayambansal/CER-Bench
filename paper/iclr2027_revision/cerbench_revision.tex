\begin{abstract}
Scientific retrieval benchmarks use relevance judgments to turn evidence sets into
system rankings. We examine how those judgments affect conclusions in CER-Bench,
a collection of 304 synthetic questions spanning eight biomedical retrieval
requirements. In the historical saved rankings, changing from source-seed labels
to pooled model judgments produces nine strict pairwise rank reversals among ten
systems at Recall@20, with Kendall's $\tau_b=0.584$ and Spearman's $\rho=0.736$.
These are calculations over saved identifier strings, not a determination of the
biologically correct system ordering. A stricter provenance audit also identifies
conflicting document identities, mismatched article attachments, and a missing
relevant pair in the stored expanded labels. We therefore separate arithmetic
reproducibility from evidence validity. We provide tested, fail-closed evaluation
contracts for structural evidence completion, selective retrieval, component
splits, and candidate-budget controls. Independent human relevance judgments,
fresh held-out queries, and identity-corrected retrieval runs remain prerequisites
for confirmatory claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Finding papers on a topic does not ensure that a retrieved set satisfies a
scientific question. A comparison needs evidence for both sides; a contradiction
requires opposing findings in interpretable conditions; and an evidence chain may
fail if a necessary link is absent. Yet these requirements are often reduced to
document membership in a relevance set.

Relevance-set construction is itself a source of uncertainty. Pooling addresses
incomplete judgments without making a collection exhaustive
\citep{buckley2004incomplete}; assessor variation can also change evaluation
\citep{voorhees2000judgments}. Synthetic questions introduce an additional
dependency when the papers used to write a question are subsequently treated as
its gold evidence. Using the same model family for generation and expanded
judgments does not provide an independent reference standard
\citep{zheng2023judge}.

Our motivating question is therefore not whether one retrieval architecture wins
on one label set. It is: \emph{how stable is the architecture comparison to the
construction and validation of those labels?} CER-Bench provides a diagnostic
case study, but the historical corpus does not currently justify confirmatory
biomedical conclusions. This revision makes that distinction explicit rather
than retaining an unsupported claim of agent superiority.

\section{Historical Collection and Evidence Audit}
The historical collection contains 4,936 corpus records and 17,902 chunk records,
not that many unique validated identities. Only 4,923 document identifiers and
17,770 chunk identifiers are distinct. The saved text-availability flags indicate
652 fulltext-bearing records and 4,284 abstract-only records. These are historical
flags, not certification of correct article attachment.

The 304 questions are balanced across constraint, comparative, contradiction,
abstention, multi-hop, temporal, aggregation, and negative-result families.
Original train/development/test counts are 119/60/125. Retrieval means use the
108 test questions with nonempty seed labels; 17 empty-label questions are
separate proxy unsupportedness cases. Reference answers are not scored.

\paragraph{Identity is an upstream requirement.}
Ten duplicated document identifiers refer to conflicting PMID/title records.
Those identifiers occur at 174 ranked positions across twelve saved methods,
affecting 44 test questions. Independently, 58 corpus records disagree with the
PMID in the attached local XML article front. This is evidence of incorrect local
attachment, not merely inconsistent formatting. Dictionary loading by a duplicated
identifier can overwrite the record used to display a retrieved hit. We cannot
recover a trustworthy producing experiment by choosing the first or last record.

\paragraph{Conservative repair candidate.}
A separate local candidate uses canonical PMID names and retains only
unambiguously attributable abstract chunks. It strips all full text, excludes
tasks touching unsafe evidence, and records every exclusion. It retains 4,936
metadata records, 4,856 abstract chunks, and 238 tasks; 80 metadata records lack
retained chunks. This is a lossy candidate, not an equivalent benchmark or a
validated full-text collection. Historical rankings are not remapped into it.

\paragraph{Split integrity is necessary but insufficient.}
The original test overlaps train/development evidence and contains two repeated
questions. Component reassignment of the conservative candidate yields
94/49/95 tasks, grouping annotated evidence and exact/lexical question duplicates.
All implemented overlap checks pass. However, these questions were already
generated and examined. Post-hoc reassignment cannot create an unexposed
confirmatory test set, and no semantic-cluster independence claim follows from
the absence of explicit cluster annotations.

\section{Relevance Regimes and Historical Arithmetic}
\paragraph{Seed labels.}
The sampled construction papers become seed-relevant identifiers, with 264
query--document pairs across the 108 supported test questions. Membership in the
generation context does not independently prove necessity or sufficiency for the
final question.

\paragraph{Pooled labels.}
The saved adjudication file contains 3,240 binary model judgments: 1,106 normalize
to relevant and 2,134 to not relevant. We normalize \texttt{NOT\_RELEVANT} before
\texttt{RELEVANT} and reject unknown verdicts. Seed retention plus these judgments
reconstructs 1,370 expanded pairs, whereas the saved expanded file contains 1,369.
The omitted pair is query \texttt{multihop\_0189}, document \texttt{37307965}; its
verdict contains explanatory text after the relevant prefix. Both historical
versions are retained and reported, not silently substituted.

\paragraph{Cluster proxies.}
The earlier v2 construction labels topic-cluster members before independently
verifying each paper against the final question. These labels are not a second
gold standard. We do not use the historical v2 contrast as confirmation of the
correct biomedical ordering in this revision.

\section{Capability Satisfaction Requires Set-level Evaluation}
For question $q$, let $U_q$ be an independently annotated set of required evidence
units, and $U_q(d)$ the units supported by document $d$. For retrieved set $R_k$,
\begin{align}
\operatorname{UnitCoverage}@k &=
\frac{|U_q\cap\bigcup_{d\in R_k} U_q(d)|}{|U_q|},\\
\operatorname{SetCompletion}@k &=
\mathbb{1}\!\left[U_q\subseteq\bigcup_{d\in R_k}U_q(d)\right].
\end{align}
These union metrics remain insufficient for relational requirements. We therefore
define family success separately: same-document constraint conjunction unless a
set-level policy is explicitly annotated; both comparison sides; completion of
an adjudicated contradiction pair; completion of one valid multi-hop path;
required temporal bins; required study--value units; and an explicitly annotated
negative-result hit. Combining halves of incompatible paths must not pass.

The implementation requires explicit evidence provenance and human/expert
annotation status. Missing annotations yield \emph{not evaluated}, not zero or
an inferred role assignment. Optional proxy scores are reported separately.
Synthetic fixtures verify metric behavior but are not empirical capability results.

\section{Candidate-budget and Selective Retrieval Protocols}
\paragraph{Do not attribute exposure gains to reasoning.}
Increasing the historical controller from one to three rounds also changes its
candidate exposure. Moreover, the released script's defaults and available
traces do not uniquely establish the manuscript's historical 3-by-8 protocol.
We withdraw the inference that ``iteration is the mechanism.''

The new protocol compares original-query top-24, repeated original-query 3-by-8,
heuristic expansion, one-shot LLM rewriting, three upfront queries without
feedback, evidence-conditioned iterative refinement, and refinement with only
the latest round's evidence. Every condition is capped at 24 unique candidate
documents; the final top-20 uses a common original-query selector, with RRF as a
diagnostic. Candidate equality is not equality of compute: observations, calls,
tokens, truncation, and failures must also be reported. RM3 must be separately
implemented and identified, not used as a name for a heuristic expander.

These conditions are implemented and fixture-tested, but live experiments have
not been run. The 62-task legacy eight-shard run is incomplete and unbound to the
new protocol. It cannot be resumed as if it were a provenance-matched run.

\paragraph{Abstention is not empty-gold recall.}
Standard rankers return rankings; this alone is not a meaningful failure to
perform calibrated abstention. We distinguish \texttt{ANSWER}, \texttt{ABSTAIN},
and \texttt{UNCERTAIN}. Coverage is the fraction answered; selective risk is the
externally judged evidence-set failure rate among answers. If answer losses are
missing, full selective risk is undefined. Score ties enter coverage curves as
blocks. Threshold selection uses development data only and is explicitly
empirical, not a conformal or population-risk guarantee. Human-verified absence
requires a documented search protocol and cannot be inferred from a finite set
of unsuccessful retrieved candidates.

\section{Confirmatory Experiments Still Required}
No confirmatory result is claimed in this section. Before submission clearance,
the following evidence is required:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Adjudicate source identities, restore and validate full text, rebuild
indices, and freeze corpus/label/index revisions.
\item Lock genuinely new evidence components and human-written queries before
method selection; keep human reference labels outside controller inputs.
\item Obtain independent, blinded biomedical judgments from two annotators,
with disagreement adjudication, evidence spans, structural roles and agreement
statistics. Include construction seeds, major-system pools and out-of-pool checks.
\item Run complete candidate-budget controls across unrelated controller
families with per-call observations and failures. Calibrate on development data.
\item Recompute all-method document, structural, and selective metrics;
evaluate rank reversals and label-completeness sensitivity; stratify on validated
text availability and independent query origin.
\end{enumerate}
The provisional local annotation kit and 80 blank human-query slots are
preparation artifacts only. A broad top-20 union is much larger than 20 papers
per question; workload and budget must be assessed before collection. No human
annotators or new external model/compute runs are currently authorized.

\section{Discussion}
The strongest surviving observation is a statement about historical evaluation
arithmetic: relevance construction changes the reported winner. The current
evidence does not identify the correct biomedical ranking or establish a causal
advantage of adaptive retrieval. Source identity, label construction, candidate
exposure, and capability satisfaction must be audited separately. Passing a
software test or reproducing a table does not validate the evidence behind it.
The revised workflow makes these boundaries executable and blocks packaging
until a separately reviewed release replaces the compromised snapshot.
